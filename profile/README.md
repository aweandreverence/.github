# Awe & Reverence

Awe & Reverence builds software for worship, Scripture engagement, prayer, and Christian community.

We care about technology that is beautiful, durable, and theologically grounded — tools that help churches, families, and everyday believers draw nearer to God rather than simply add more noise to their lives.

## What we are building

- **[Awesome.Bible](https://awesome.bible)** — a modern Bible study platform for reading, searching, saving, memorizing, sharing, and exploring Scripture.
- **[Maskil](https://maskil.church)** — a digital hymnal and worship study platform, with songs, languages, annotations, and Scripture connections.
- **[Phileo.church](https://phileo.church)** — a church directory and membership/community platform, preparing for launch.
- **[LetsPray.AI](https://letspray.ai)** — an AI-assisted prayer app, preparing for launch.
- **Faith-focused web projects** — public resources for evangelism, theology, church discovery, compliance, and discipleship.

## Open source at A&R

We publish and maintain parts of our work here so others can learn from it, build on it, and contribute where it fits.

A few public places to start:

- [www.aweandreverence.com](https://github.com/aweandreverence/www.aweandreverence.com) — our public company site.
- [www.sdg.church](https://github.com/aweandreverence/www.sdg.church) — Soli Deo Gloria, an evangelism and discipleship site.
- [www.about.church](https://github.com/aweandreverence/www.about.church) — a guide for finding a faithful local church.
- [www.ab506.church](https://github.com/aweandreverence/www.ab506.church) — a practical resource for California church child-protection compliance.
- [TheGospelJohn316](https://github.com/aweandreverence/TheGospelJohn316) — a cross-platform Gospel-sharing app.

We also work in partnership with [Hacktoolkit](https://github.com/hacktoolkit) patterns and tooling, including Django, Expo, CSS, and shell foundations used across our product work.

Some active product repositories are private while they are under development. Public-facing product work lands here when it is ready to be shared.

## Our posture

We are Christian builders. That means the details matter: accessibility, reliability, thoughtful design, responsible data use, careful licensing, and reverence for the content we handle.

If you are exploring this organization, welcome. We are building in public where we can, learning as we go, and trying to make software that serves the church well.
